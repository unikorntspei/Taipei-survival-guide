Chapter 03

The Cheat Sheet這些事沒人告訴你，但你需要知道

Things nobody tells you, but you really need to know
注意事項 Must-Know Rules
⚠ 捷運禁食 MRT No Food — 禁止飲食、喝水（含口香糖），違者罰NT$1,500–7,500。No eating, drinking or gum on the MRT — fines up to NT$7,500.
🚽 公廁無衛生紙 No Toilet Paper — 公廁不一定有衛生紙，出門記得自備。Public restrooms may not have toilet paper — always carry some.
💰 不用給小費 No Tipping — 台灣沒有給小費文化，給了反而尷尬。No tipping expected anywhere — it can feel awkward if you try.
生活文化 Local Life
🏪
便利商店
Convenience Stores
24小時、可繳費、可加熱食物、有座位、有ATM、可寄包裹、可影印。密度全球前二，平均1,703人就有一間。
24/7, pay bills, hot food, seating, ATM, parcel pickup, photocopying. Taiwan ranks top 2 globally in store density.
🧋
手搖飲文化
Bubble Tea Culture
每條街都有，可客製甜度和冰量。這是日常，不是觀光景點。
On every block. Customise sweetness & ice level. Daily life — not a tourist attraction.
🌙
夜市文化
Night Market
不只是吃飯，是台灣的一種生活方式。晚上7點後去，最熱鬧。
More than food — it's a lifestyle. Go after 7pm for the full experience.
🚛
垃圾車音樂
Musical Garbage Trucks
每天播放〈少女的祈禱〉，聽到音樂就要追著跑去丟垃圾。這傳統從1968年開始。
Plays "Maiden's Prayer" daily since 1968. When you hear it — that's your cue to run out and toss your trash.
🥣
早餐店
Breakfast Shops
台灣人很少自己煮。早上5點到下午2點，供應蛋餅、飯糰、豆漿等各式中式及西式餐點，便宜又飽。
Taiwanese rarely cook at home. Open 5am–2pm, serving egg crepes, rice balls, soy milk, and a wide mix of local and Western-style dishes — cheap and filling.
🥡
打包文化
Taking Food To-Go
吃不完直接跟店家說「我要打包」，完全正常，不需要不好意思。外帶也很普遍，很多店家都有提供打包袋。
Asking to pack up your leftovers is completely normal — just say "我要打包" (wǒ yào dǎ bāo). Takeaway is widely available everywhere.
📱
捷運可以講電話
MRT Phone Etiquette
禁止飲食（包含喝水），但可以講電話——跟日本完全相反，初次來台容易搞混。
No eating or drinking (including water), but phone calls are fine — the opposite of Japan. Easy to mix up on your first visit.
🏍️
機車文化
Scooter Culture
街上機車很多，過馬路務必注意左右，尤其是右轉機車。
Lots of scooters — look carefully both ways, especially for right-turning scooters.
🍜
吃冰配熱湯
Ice + Hot Soup Together
同一餐同時點刨冰和熱湯，完全正常，不要驚訝。
Ordering shaved ice and hot soup in one meal? Totally normal. Don't be surprised.
🌛
宵夜文化
Late Night Food
台灣人非常喜歡吃宵夜，這點跟很多國家不太一樣。最經典的選擇是永和豆漿——類似24小時的早餐店，半夜也能吃到豆漿、蛋餅、燒餅。
Taiwanese love late-night eating. The go-to is Yonghe Soy Milk — a 24hr breakfast-style shop serving soy milk, egg crepes & sesame flatbreads at any hour.
🧾
發票 × 載具
Receipts & E-Invoice
台灣每筆消費都會開立統一發票，還可以綁定「載具」自動儲存。每兩個月還有機會中獎。持手機或悠遊卡就能當載具用。
Every purchase issues a government receipt. Link a "carrier" (your phone or EasyCard) to store them digitally — and enter a bimonthly lottery with cash prizes.
🏥
全民健保
National Health Insurance
台灣有全民健保制度，看診費用對本國人來說非常便宜。外國旅客雖然不在健保範圍內，但台灣整體醫療水準高、費用相對合理。
Taiwan has universal healthcare — extremely affordable for locals. Visitors aren't covered, but medical standards are high and costs are reasonable.
💴
付錢給整數
Round Up When Paying
在台灣消費時，習慣給整數讓找零方便。例如消費83元，通常給103元而不是精確湊零錢。這是普遍的消費禮儀。
When paying, it's common to hand over a round number — e.g. NT$103 for an NT$83 item. Counting out exact change is unusual.
📺
電視全都有字幕
TV Always Has Subtitles
包括台語節目，幾乎所有電視節目都有中文字幕，有助學中文。
Almost all TV shows have Chinese subtitles, including Taiwanese-language programs.
🚪
進房間前先敲門
Knock Before Entering
台灣有個不成文的習慣：進飯店房間前會先敲門，即使是自己的房間。這源自於對「房間裡可能有其他存在」的民間信仰，是一種表示尊重的禮節。
It's common to knock before entering a hotel room — even your own. Rooted in folk belief that spaces may have unseen presences, it's considered a respectful gesture.
🚶
小綠人紅綠燈
The Walking Man Traffic Light
台灣發明了會動的行人號誌「小綠人」。綠燈時小人會走動（越走越快代表快要紅燈了），紅燈時停止。這項設計後來被許多國家引進。
Taiwan invented the animated pedestrian signal. The green figure walks (speeding up as time runs out) and stops on red. This design has since been adopted worldwide.
🍬
乖乖文化
The Kuai Kuai Ritual
在台灣，你可能會在機器、伺服器、ATM上看到一包綠色「奶油椰子」口味的乖乖零食。台灣人相信放了它，機器就會「乖乖」正常運作。沒有人能解釋，但大家都這樣做。
You may spot a green "Kuai Kuai" coconut snack placed on top of machines, servers, or ATMs. Taiwanese believe it keeps equipment running smoothly. No one knows why. Everyone does it anyway.
